# Project technology logos

Retrieved on 2026-11-18. Assets are served locally in their brand colors at 20px.
The Next.js mark switches from black to white on dark surfaces. OpenSearch and
NGXS are brightened on dark surfaces for visibility.

- The Simple Icons assets in this directory cover Go, Flutter, OpenSearch, Kubernetes, Next.js, Spring,
  Spring Boot, Hibernate, Jenkins, Angular, Svelte, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS,
  Kotlin, Swift, Jetpack Compose, and Supabase. SVGs are from
  `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/`.
  Simple Icons assets use CC0. Brand names and marks belong to their owners.
  Kotlin, Swift, and Svelte marks represent Kotlin Multiplatform, SwiftUI, and
  SvelteKit respectively.
  Brand fill colors come from
  `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/data/simple-icons.json`.
- Flutter, Kubernetes, Hibernate, Jenkins, Angular, Spring, Svelte, and TypeScript
  reuse the existing full-color SVG assets under `static/assets/skills/`.
- NGXS uses its official SVG wordmark.
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ngxs/store/master/docs/assets/ngxs-horizontal.svg
- NG-ZORRO uses its official mark.
  https://ng.ant.design/assets/img/logo.svg

Echo, SQL, CI/CD, and Provider have no displayed logo. Unmapped technologies are omitted.
